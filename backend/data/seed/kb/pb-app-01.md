# Authorised Push Payment Scam

The customer authorises the transfer themselves after being persuaded (fake investment, fake sale, romance scam). Device and PIN are normal; key signals: amount far above usual, new beneficiary with a young account, high hesitation.

**Decision:** amount > 10× average to a new beneficiary = at least HOLD and CHALLENGE (confirm transfer purpose). DECLINE if the customer confirms they were persuaded by a third party.
