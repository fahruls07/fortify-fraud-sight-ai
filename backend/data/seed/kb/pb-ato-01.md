# Account Takeover

The fraudster logs into the customer's account from a new or rooted device, often after leaked credentials. Traits: new device, different typing rhythm, unusual hours, several PIN failures.

**Decision:** new device + new beneficiary + high amount = HIGH. Action: CHALLENGE with additional verification; if it fails, DECLINE.
