# Phishing / Credential Theft

The customer enters credentials on a fake site or link; the fraudster uses them from another device. Traits: login from a new device shortly after a phishing report, new beneficiary, transfer outside active hours.

**Decision:** CHALLENGE and ask the customer to change credentials; if there are signs of takeover, HOLD and escalate.
